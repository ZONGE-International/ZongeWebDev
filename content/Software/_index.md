        </tr>
        <tr>

        <tr>
          <td>SCSINV</td>
          <td>Runs smooth-model 1D <a href="/methods/groundmethods/csamt/">CSAMT</a> inversion to build stitched resistivity-depth images along a line.</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>STEMINV</td>
          <td>Performs smooth-model 1D inversion of <a href="/methods/groundmethods/tem/">TEM</a> soundings into layered-earth resistivity models.</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>ASTATIC</td>
          <td>Applies <a href="/methods/groundmethods/mt/">MT</a> static correction and converts survey outputs into formats such as SEG and EDI.</td>
