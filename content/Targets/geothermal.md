## Geothermal Project Experience

### Newberry Volcano, Oregon

Zonge International conducted [gravity surveys](/methods/groundmethods/gravity/) at Newberry Volcano in 2007 and 2010 for Davenport Newberry, followed by a [magnetotelluric (MT) survey](/methods/groundmethods/mt/) in 2011. The combined interpretation drew on more than 100 MT stations from Geosystems’ 2006 survey and Zonge’s 2011 survey, together with gravity data and drill-hole lithology, to refine the subsurface model and evaluate the area's hydrothermal potential.

The study suggests that shallow conductive zones reflect clay-altered volcanic deposits within earlier eruptive craters. This interpretation combines the MT response with geological context rather than treating conductivity alone as evidence of a geothermal reservoir.

Read the 2013 Stanford Geothermal Workshop paper, [*The Evolving Role of MT in Geothermal Exploration at Newberry Volcano, Oregon*](/documents/newberry-volcano-mt-geothermal-exploration.pdf) (PDF, 1.1 MB).

### Steptoe Valley, Nevada

As part of a Sandia National Laboratories-led geothermal research project, Zonge International conducted a combined 40-station MT and CSEM survey in September–October 2022 and processed the resulting data in northern Steptoe Valley, Nevada. The public overview presents the broader investigation and its findings: [Geophysical Imaging at Steptoe Valley, Nevada](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/fdba3f1084b7469b87d4f6822c60311c).
