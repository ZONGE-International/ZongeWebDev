## Ivy Project, Nevada · 2025 {#ivy-project-nevada-2025}

At Carlin Gold’s Ivy project in Nevada, Zonge conducted DC IP/resistivity surveying and inversion modeling in 2025. Carlin Gold reported chargeability anomalies beneath the Main Skarn Zone and a deeper, adjacent response, helping define targets for further exploration.

The survey used six lines with 75 m dipoles; three of those lines were also surveyed with 200 m dipoles. Five lines crossed the Main Skarn Zone.

See the client’s [November 2025 survey results](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/274279/Carlin-Golds-Ivy-CopperGold-Project-DC-ResistivityIP-Survey-Targets-Identified) and [Ivy project maps and survey details](https://carlingold.com/projects/ivy/).
