## Overview

The ZT-100B is a rack-mount transmitter rated at 100 A and 1,200 V. A separate switched-mode power supply (SMPS) connects it to a standard 50 or 60 Hz generator, giving crews a choice of power configurations.

<div class="precision-power-options"><div><h3>110–120 V</h3><p>Single-phase input for a portable, low-power setup.</p></div><div><h3>240 V</h3><p>Single-phase input with the ZPS12-1200 power supply.</p></div><div><h3>Three-phase</h3><p>Input from a larger generator for higher-power applications.</p></div></div>

Zonge can help select a power supply and package it with the switching unit in a rugged case.

## FAQ

**Does the ZT-100B connect directly to a generator?**

No. A separate SMPS is required. Contact Zonge to select a power supply for your survey requirements.

**Is the ZT-100B compatible with my old Zonge generator?**

The system is intended for standard 50 or 60 Hz generators. Earlier Zonge generators operate at 400 Hz; discuss compatibility with Zonge before selecting a power supply.

**Can it synchronize with a GDP, ZEN, or another GPS receiver?**

It supports GPS synchronization with ZEN and GDP receivers. Confirm timing compatibility with Zonge when using other receivers.
