Zonge designs and supports geophysical instrumentation for electrical, electromagnetic, and potential-field surveys. Our systems are built for field crews who need reliable acquisition, controlled-source power, and practical support from people who understand survey operations.

## Receivers

- [GDP3224](/instruments/rx/gdp3224/) - 24-bit receiver platform for multi-channel geophysical data acquisition.
- [ZEN](/instruments/rx/zen/) - compact receiver system for field geophysical applications.

## Transmitters

- [ZT-100B](/instruments/tx/zt100/) - high-power geophysical transmitter for modern controlled-source surveys.
- [XMT-R](/instruments/tx/xmtr/) - transmitter controller with local controls and remote web-interface operation.
- [GGT-10B](/instruments/tx/ggt10/) - generator/transmitter package for geophysical field work.
- [GGT-30B](/instruments/tx/ggt30/) - higher-power generator/transmitter package for demanding survey configurations.
- [ZT-30B](/instruments/tx/zt30b/) - geophysical transmitter for established Zonge survey workflows.

## Generators

- [ZMG-9](/instruments/generators/zmg9/) - generator package for geophysical transmitter systems.
- [ZMG-30DLE](/instruments/generators/zmg30/) - legacy 30 KVA diesel generator package, not currently available for new deployments.
